OUR INSPIRATION

History, in its own right.

Real historical figures and genuine archival imagery — referenced accurately, never used to imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Respect for the record.

History is richer than a single portrait or slogan. Our wider visual language draws on heraldry, the English rose, the Welsh dragon, the Scottish thistle and Northern Ireland's flax-flower tradition. Military-inspired design means original artwork — not replicas of protected regimental badges, official honours or the Royal Arms.

Our historical image sources and reuse decisions are documented for editorial review before launch.
